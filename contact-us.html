FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Common questions,
clear answers.

Find answers to common questions about bookings, changes, baggage, and refunds.

How can I change my booking?

You can request changes to your booking by contacting our support team. Availability and fare differences may apply depending on your ticket type and airline policy.

What is your baggage policy?

Your baggage allowance is shown in your itinerary and depends on the airline and fare you selected.

How do I request a refund?

Contact us with your booking reference. We’ll explain the applicable fare rules and next steps.

How long does a refund take?

Timing depends on airline approval and your payment provider. Our team can help you check the request status.

Can I change the name on my ticket?

Name correction rules vary by airline. Contact support as soon as possible so we can review your ticket conditions.
